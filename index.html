More than a list of achievements.

I once measured progress by awards. Over time, photography, research, teamwork, and service taught me that the more meaningful questions are what I learned, how I responded to uncertainty, and what I contributed to others.

Where curiosity began

A camera opened a bigger question.

Taking apart an old digital camera introduced me to the silicon image sensor inside it. I had used it to capture hundreds of photographs without understanding how light could become electrical information. That small discovery led me from photography toward chemistry, materials, and semiconductor technology.

Looking ahead

From research to reliable technology.

I want to study how material composition, surfaces, interfaces, and processing conditions influence the performance and reliability of electronic devices. My long-term goal is to grow into an R&D or materials engineer who can help connect promising research to practical manufacturing, including collaboration between Taiwan and Vietnam.

Research direction: materials, characterization, surfaces & interfaces
